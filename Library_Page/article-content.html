How Climate Change Affects Indonesia’s Ecosystems
Indonesia is one of the most biodiverse countries in the world, home to lush rainforests, vibrant coral reefs, and countless unique species. However, climate change is putting immense pressure on these ecosystems, threatening both wildlife and local communities.
Rising Temperatures and Changing Weather Patterns
Indonesia’s average temperature has been steadily increasing, leading to longer dry seasons and more extreme weather events. These changes disrupt delicate ecosystems, making it harder for plants and animals to survive.
